My Favourite Hobby: Photography

Photography is one of my favourite hobbies because it allows me to capture interesting moments and beautiful places. I enjoy taking pictures when I am outside, especially when the weather is nice. Photography is also a good way to be creative. You can take pictures of people, animals, buildings, nature, or everyday objects. You do not need an expensive camera to get started because a phone can also take great photographs. Learning photography can teach you about light, colours, angles, and composition. It is a relaxing hobby that I can enjoy with friends and family.

My Favourite Things to Photograph

  • Nature
  • Animals
  • Sunsets
  • Buildings
  • Family and friends
